Graphing Calculator

Plot up to three functions at once. Drag to move, scroll or pinch buttons to zoom.

How to use the graphing calculator

Type a formula in terms of x into any of the three boxes and the curve is drawn straight away. Drag the graph to move around and use the mouse wheel or the zoom buttons to see more or less detail. Move your pointer over the graph to read the exact x and y coordinates under it.

Things to try

  • x^2 draws a parabola. Add x^2 - 4 to see it shift down by 4 and cross the x-axis at −2 and 2.
  • sin(x) and cos(x) together show how the two waves are shifted by a quarter turn.
  • 1/x shows a hyperbola with a break at x = 0.
  • abs(x) gives a V shape, and sqrt(x) only exists for x ≥ 0.

Reading a graph

Where a curve crosses the x-axis, the function equals zero. These points are called roots or zeros and they are the solutions of the equation f(x) = 0. Where it crosses the y-axis is the y-intercept, found by putting x = 0. Peaks and valleys are local maximum and minimum points, and where two curves cross, both functions have the same value, which solves an equation such as x² = 2x + 3.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is part of my graph missing?

Some functions are not defined everywhere. For example sqrt(x) and ln(x) need positive values, and 1/x is undefined at 0. The calculator leaves gaps where the result does not exist.

Can I plot an equation like y = 2x + 1?

Yes, type just 2x + 1. A leading y= is also accepted.

How do I zoom on a phone?

Use the Zoom in and Zoom out buttons below the graph, and drag with one finger to move the view.
