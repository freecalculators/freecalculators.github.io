About Us

Who we are and why we build free calculators.

Our mission

CalcZone provides free, fast and easy-to-use online calculators for students, professionals and anyone who needs a quick answer. We believe a good calculator should load instantly, work on any device, be clear about how it reaches its answer and never ask you to sign up.

What we offer

  • A full scientific calculator with trigonometry, logarithms and history.
  • A simple standard calculator for everyday sums.
  • A fractions calculator with step-by-step working.
  • A graphing calculator for plotting functions.
  • A percentage calculator for discounts, marks and growth.
  • A fun love calculator for entertainment.

How we work

All calculations run directly in your web browser. Your numbers are never uploaded to a server. We test our formulas against known results and explain them on each page so you can understand, not just copy, an answer. We are adding new calculators regularly, so please check back often.
