About the standard calculator

The standard calculator covers the four basic operations plus brackets, which is everything most people need for shopping totals, bills, homework checks and quick estimates. It follows the normal order of operations, so multiplication and division are done before addition and subtraction. You can type on your keyboard or tap the buttons.

Tips for accurate results

  • Use brackets when the order matters. (5+3)×2 is 16, while 5+3×2 is 11.
  • Decimals such as 0.1 + 0.2 are rounded sensibly, so you get 0.3 instead of a long string of digits.
  • Check the history (top left icon) to review or reuse earlier results.
  • Need roots, powers or trigonometry? Open the scientific calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate with negative numbers?

Yes. Press the minus key before a number, for example −5+8, or after another operator, for example 6×−2.

What happens if I divide by zero?

Division by zero has no answer, so the display shows Error. Press AC and start again.

Is there a limit on the number size?

Very large or very small results are shown in scientific notation, for example 1.5E21.
