Fractions Calculator

Add, subtract, multiply and divide fractions and mixed numbers. See the simplified answer, the mixed number, the decimal and every step.

How to use the fractions calculator

Enter the numerator (top number) and denominator (bottom number) of each fraction. If you have a mixed number such as 2 ¾, type 2 in the whole-number box, 3 in the numerator and 4 in the denominator. Choose an operation and the result updates instantly, along with the working.

Rules for fraction arithmetic

Adding and subtracting
Fractions need a common denominator. Find the least common multiple (LCM) of the two denominators, rewrite both fractions, then add or subtract the numerators. Example: 1/2 + 1/3 = 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.
Multiplying
Multiply the numerators together and the denominators together. Example: 2/3 × 3/4 = 6/12 = 1/2.
Dividing
Flip the second fraction (take its reciprocal) and multiply. Example: 1/2 ÷ 1/4 = 1/2 × 4/1 = 4/2 = 2.
Simplifying
Divide the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor (GCD). For 18/24 the GCD is 6, so the simplest form is 3/4.

Improper fractions and mixed numbers

An improper fraction has a numerator larger than its denominator, such as 7/4. A mixed number combines a whole number and a proper fraction, so 7/4 is the same as 1 ¾. To convert a mixed number to an improper fraction, multiply the whole number by the denominator, add the numerator and keep the denominator: 2 ¾ becomes (2×4+3)/4 = 11/4. The calculator shows both forms so you can use whichever your teacher or recipe needs.

Worked example

Add 3/4 and 5/6. The LCM of 4 and 6 is 12, so 3/4 becomes 9/12 and 5/6 becomes 10/12. Adding gives 19/12, which is already in lowest terms. As a mixed number that is 1 7/12, and as a decimal about 1.5833.

Where fractions are used

Fractions appear in cooking measurements, carpentry and sewing, probability, ratios, music timing and almost every level of school mathematics. Being comfortable with them makes percentages and decimals much easier too.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I enter a negative fraction?

Yes. Put the minus sign in the numerator, for example −3 over 4. For a negative mixed number, type a negative whole number.

Why does it say the denominator cannot be zero?

A fraction with zero in the denominator is undefined, because you cannot split something into zero equal parts.

Does it always simplify the answer?

Yes. The final answer is always shown in lowest terms, with the mixed number and decimal beside it.
