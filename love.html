Love Calculator

Enter two names and see your fun love score. Just for entertainment!

What is the love calculator?

The love calculator is a playful tool that turns two names into a “love percentage”. It is a popular party game and a fun way to start a conversation with friends. The score is produced by a simple formula that mixes the letters of both names, so the same two names always give the same result, whichever order you type them in.

Just for fun: this calculator is entertainment only. It has no scientific basis and cannot measure or predict real feelings or compatibility. Please do not take the result seriously.

What really makes relationships work

Psychologists who study long-lasting relationships often point to a few habits that matter far more than any score:

  • Honest communication: talk about small worries before they become big ones.
  • Respect and kindness: notice and appreciate everyday efforts.
  • Shared time: make room for activities you both enjoy.
  • Trust and support: back each other’s goals and bounce back from disagreements together.

Ideas to use the result

Try it with friends at a gathering, add a fun twist to a birthday message, or use it as an icebreaker in a group chat. If your score is low, laugh about it. If it is high, enjoy the compliment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the love percentage accurate?

No. It is a game. The percentage is calculated from the letters in the two names and is not a real measure of compatibility.

Why do I always get the same result?

The formula is fixed, so the same pair of names always gives the same score. Try a nickname or a full name for a different result.

Do you store the names I enter?

No. The calculation happens in your browser and the names are not sent anywhere.
